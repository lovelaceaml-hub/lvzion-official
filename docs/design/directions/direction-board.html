Three looks for the whole LV Zion app

Each option sets the colors, type, spacing and layout that every part of the app will share: Home, sign in, Work Mission prayer, Study Rooms, Church Display, Contacts and Worship Duty. All names and times below are made-up examples.

  • Phone first
  • Tap the header to go home
  • Swipe between tabs
  • No church imagery, no emoji
  • Large tap targets, readable contrast

A. Day Sheet

Recommended

Home reads like a printed order of the day: a single time line from morning to night with a "now" mark, and your apps underneath. Calm, orderly and easy to read at any age.

Feels
Calm, orderly, unhurried
Signature
The day line: times on the left, a thin "now" marker that moves through the day
Type
Phone system text for everything; a quiet serif only for the greeting and page titles
Color
Soft grey-green paper, white surfaces, one deep spruce green for actions

  • Members of every age: plain lists, big readable times
  • Every module can use it easily (lists and forms, very little color to manage)
  • Closest to today's green, so the change feels familiar

Gives up

Will never be

  • Gradients, glow or decorative circles
  • Pill badges on everything

B. Module Board

Apps first

Home is a board of your apps. Each app owns one muted color that follows it everywhere, so Prayer always looks like Prayer. Your next commitment sits on top in a dark block.

Feels
Direct, confident, quick to scan
Signature
One color per app, carried into that app's own screens
Type
Heavy system sans, large and tight, no serif
Color
Warm stone ground, near-black blocks, five muted app colors

C. First Light

Dark first

Built for the early hours: a dark, low-glare screen where the next thing on your day is shown as a large clock with a countdown. A matching light version would follow the phone's setting.

Feels
Focused, quiet, low-glare
Signature
The big rounded clock and countdown bar for whatever is next
Type
Rounded numerals for times, small spaced capitals for labels
Color
Deep blue-slate night, one warm dawn amber for what matters now

  • 5 AM prayer without a bright screen

  • The most striking and memorable

My recommendation: A, Day Sheet.

It is the easiest to read for every member, it lets the five apps share one quiet system without each inventing its own colors, and it keeps the green LV Zion already uses. B is the better pick if you want the app to feel like a launcher; C if early-morning use matters most.
